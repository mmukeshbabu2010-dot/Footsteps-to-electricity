Footstep Power Generator System

This system converts mechanical foot pressure into regulated DC electricity using a 10x piezoelectric disc array housed inside a spring-loaded mechanical footstep frame. AC voltage spikes are rectified through a 4x 1N4007 diode bridge, smoothed by a 100µF capacitor, and processed by an Arduino Nano. Step count and generated voltage are displayed via an I2C-driven 16x2 LCD screen, while a BC547 NPN transistor drives a high-brightness LED for visual pulse confirmation.
