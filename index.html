Footstep Power Generator System

This project captures kinetic mechanical energy generated by human footsteps and converts it into usable electrical energy using an array of piezoelectric sensor discs. The generated voltage is rectified through a full-wave bridge diode network, smoothed by a capacitor, and processed by an Arduino Nano. The system monitors live step counts and voltage output on a 16x2 LCD screen, triggering an LED feedback indicator on every step.
