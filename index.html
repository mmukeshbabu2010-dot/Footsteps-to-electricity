Hardware Wiring & Coordinate Guide

1. I2C LCD Display Module

  • LCD VCC → Arduino 5V Rail
  • LCD GND → Arduino GND Rail
  • LCD SDA → Arduino Nano Pin A4
  • LCD SCL → Arduino Nano Pin A5

2. Full-Wave Bridge Rectifier (1N4007 Diodes)

Converts the AC voltage spikes from mechanical foot pressure into unidirectional DC pulses.

  • Diode 1 & 2 Anodes → Ground (-) Rail
  • Diode 3 & 4 Cathodes → Capacitor (+) & Arduino Pin A0
  • Piezo Leads → AC junction points between Diodes

3. BC547 Transistor & LED Indicator Switch

  • Base (Middle Pin) → 1kΩ Resistor → Arduino Digital Pin 13
  • Collector (Right Pin) → Ground (-) Rail
  • Emitter (Left Pin) → Blue LED Cathode (-)
  • Blue LED Anode (+) → 220Ω Resistor → 5V Rail
